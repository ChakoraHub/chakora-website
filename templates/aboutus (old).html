About the Founder

This website has been founded by V. Subhash Chandra, a graduate in engineering from the class of 2011. Over the years, he has gained professional experience across various multinational corporations such as TCS, Cognizant, and Wipro. He is currently engaged as an Online trainer at Radical Technologies, sharing his knowledge and expertise with aspiring professionals.

In March 2020, he received brahmana initiation from Bhuvanagiri Venkata Subrahmanya Sastry Garu, marking a significant spiritual milestone. His journey to pursue a spiritual life while continuing in the field of Information Technology has been deeply enriched from the very beginning by the timeless teachings of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, the Founder-Acharya of ISKCON, who appeared in the disciplic succession of the Brahma-Madhva-Gaudiya Sampradaya.

Deeply inspired by these profound Vedic teachings, he was moved to make a humble offering in the form of this website — a space intended to serve, uplift, and spiritually nourish seekers from all walks of life. May this small endeavor become a meaningful contribution for the benefit of humanity at large.

About the Company

Chakora Hub Pvt. Ltd. is a multi-domain innovation, education, and technology company with a mission to uplift society through knowledge, creativity, and meaningful problem-solving.

We specialize in:

  • Automation & Software Development
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Solutions
  • Cloud Technologies & Infrastructure Consulting
  • Master Data Management (MDM) Services
  • End-to-End IT Project Implementation & Support

Alongside services, we also provide high-quality training that bridges the gap between learning and real-world application.

Our training includes:

  • IT Technologies (Python, Cloud, DevOps, Data Engineering, etc.)
  • Automation Frameworks
  • AI / ML Project Implementation
  • Photoshop, UI/UX Fundamentals, and Digital Design

We are also expanding into Creative & Cultural Education, enabling learners to grow in both technology and art.
