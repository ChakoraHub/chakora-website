V. Subhash Chandra

Founder, ChakoraHub  ·  Engineer  ·  Educator  ·  Spiritual Seeker

A 2011 engineering graduate who has spent over a decade across India's largest IT organizations, evolved into an educator, entrepreneur, and a devotee on a mission to blend technology with timeless wisdom. This is his story.

The journey

From Campus to Calling

Nov 2011 – Dec 2013  ·  2 yrs 2 mos
Tata Consultancy Services
A.S.E Trainee Full-time
Fresh out of engineering, Subhash stepped into India's largest IT company, TCS — laying the foundation of his professional career in Hyderabad. This chapter shaped his technical discipline and introduced him to enterprise-scale software systems.
💼
🚀
Feb 2014 – Sep 2024  ·  10 yrs 8 mos
Cognizant
Sr. Associate Full-time
A decade of deep expertise at Cognizant — the longest and most formative chapter of his corporate journey. Rising to Senior Associate, he honed his skills across complex client engagements, mastering everything from automation to data management over more than ten years of dedicated service.
March 2020  ·  Spiritual Milestone
Brahmana Initiation
A Sacred Turning Point
In the midst of his corporate life, a profound spiritual milestone arrived. Subhash received brahmana initiation from Bhuvanagiri Venkata Subrahmanya Sastry Garu, marking his formal entry into the Brahma-Madhva-Gaudiya Sampradaya — the disciplic succession of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, Founder-Acharya of ISKCON. This awakening ignited the vision of ChakoraHub.
🪔
📊
Nov 2024 – May 2025  ·  7 mos
Wipro
Project Manager Full-time
A strategic chapter that elevated his career to a leadership role. As Project Manager at Wipro, Subhash led end-to-end project delivery, steering cross-functional teams and enterprise initiatives — completing the trilogy of India's top IT giants: TCS, Cognizant, and Wipro.
Feb 2025 – Present  ·  1 yr 3 mos
Radical Technologies India
Online Tutor   Full-time · Active
Channeling over a decade of industry experience into shaping the next generation. As an Online Tutor at Radical Technologies, Subhash trains aspiring professionals across Python, Cloud, DevOps, Data Engineering, AI/ML, and more — bridging the gap between classroom theory and real-world application.
🎓
🌐
Jun 2025 – Present  ·  Self-Employed
ChakoraHub
Founder Active · Remote
The culmination of a journey. ChakoraHub was born as a humble offering — a multi-domain platform weaving together technology, education, creativity, and Vedic wisdom. A space to serve, uplift, and spiritually nourish seekers from all walks of life.
Jul 2025 – Jul 2027  ·  Ongoing
BITS Pilani
MTech  ·  Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning In Progress
Never stopping to learn — Subhash is currently pursuing a Master of Technology in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from the prestigious Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani. A practitioner becoming a scholar.
